How it Works

Apply Directly or Book a Call

The summer bootcamp is the perfect roadmap for beginners wanting to learn to code. When you apply to the bootcamp we'll reach out to you to book a short digital meeting. The meeting is chance for you to ask questions about the bootcamp and the curriculum, and a chance for us to see if the bootcamp is a good fit for you.

    FAQs

    Find answers to common questions about our B2B courses, bootcamps, training programs, and more.

    What type of courses do you give?

    Right now, our Generative AI and Prompting courses are the most popular. We also teach Python, SQL, Data Visualization, Data Analytics, BI tools, and more. Our courses normally include modules on how to use Generative AI to get most out of SQL for example.

    What’s the benefit of tailor-made courses?

    We don’t build courses for the sake of teaching technology; we create courses that are relevant to your particular business. We are dedicated to upskilling your team with practical skills they will use upon finishing the course, helping your business overcome real challenges.

    Are all courses tailor-made?

    No, we also offer a broad range of pre-packaged courses. You can choose the level of customization and duration yourself.
